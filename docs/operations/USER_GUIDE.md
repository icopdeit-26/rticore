# 20. Enterprise User Guide

## Role Workspaces & Daily Workflows
1. **Executives (CEO / CFO / Directors):**
   - Open `/erp/executive-command` for entity-scoped Commercial Pipeline, Service Profitability (EBITDA view), Resource Utilization, FlexGuard Performance, Budget Variance, and Enterprise Risk.
   - Open `/finance` (`F01`–`F25`) for statutory PSAK financial reporting, closing cockpit, and consolidation.
2. **Sales & Presales:**
   - Manage Leads & Opportunities (`/sales/leads`, `/sales/pipeline`, `/customers`).
   - Use `/erp/service-catalog` to select approved 29-category service definitions and 12-phase activity templates.
   - Run `/erp/pricing-simulator` to model mandays, complexity, tools/travel, margins, discounts, and Fixed Price vs FlexGuard comparisons; submit scenarios for independent approval & immutable quotation snapshot freezing.
3. **Project Managers & Cybersecurity Delivery Teams (VAPT / SOC / DFIR / CTI / GRC):**
   - Track delivery milestones (`/sales/delivery`), daily capacity timesheets (`/psa/timesheets`), SOC/DFIR incidents (`/erp/cyber-operations`), and CTI Cases/IOCs + GRC Consulting Engagements + Enterprise Risk Register (`/erp/cyber-grc-cti`).
4. **FlexGuard Operations & Customers:**
   - Internal teams manage FlexGuard packages, conversion multipliers, 14-step service requests, credit reservations/consumption, SLA breaches, and renewal advisories at `/erp/flexguard-enterprise` and `/flexguard`.
   - Authorized customer contacts access `/portal/flexguard` to view active balances, submit requests, approve scopes, accept deliverables, and download monthly utilization statements.
