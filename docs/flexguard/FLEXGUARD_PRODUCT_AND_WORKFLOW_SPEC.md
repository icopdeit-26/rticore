# 7. FlexGuard Product & Workflow Specification (M09)

Implemented in `migrations/0030_flexguard_immutable_ledger.sql`, `migrations/0054_flexguard_enterprise_suite.sql`, `src/lib/flexguard-enterprise.ts`, `/api/erp/flexguard-enterprise`, `/api/portal/flexguard`, `/erp/flexguard-enterprise`, and `/portal/flexguard`.

## Core Capabilities
1. **Configurable Package Builder (`rticore_flexguard_packages`):** Supports Essential (20), Professional (50), Business (100), Enterprise (250), Strategic (500), and Custom templates across 8 billing models (`prepaid_mandays`, `postpaid_mandays`, `annual_credits`, `monthly_allocation`, `quarterly_allocation`, `retainer`, `committed_capacity`, `hybrid_subscription`) with maker-checker approval.
2. **Versioned Service Credit Conversion Matrix (`rticore_flexguard_conversion_matrix`):** Contractual multipliers per `(contract_id, contract_version, service_tier)` — Standard `1.00x`, Senior `1.25x`, Lead `1.50x`, Principal `2.00x`, Emergency `2.00x`, Forensic configurable. Formula: `Consumed Credits = ceil(Billable Mandays × Contractual Multiplier)`.
3. **12-Type Immutable Credit Ledger (`rticore_flexguard_enterprise_ledger`):** `initial_allocation`, `purchase`, `top_up`, `reservation`, `reservation_release`, `consumption`, `adjustment`, `reversal`, `transfer`, `carry_forward`, `expiry`, `refund`. Every transaction carries `idempotency_key`, `legal_entity_id`, `customer_id`, `actor_email`, and `reason`. DB triggers prohibit deletion and tampering. `Available Credits = Posted Credit Balance − Active Reservations`. Negative balance blocked unless approved contractual overdraft exists. Adjustments require independent approver (SoD).
4. **14-Step Service Request Workflow (`rticore_flexguard_service_requests`):** `submitted` → `scope_validated` → `estimated` → `credit_quoted` → `customer_approved` → `credits_reserved` → `resource_assigned` → `in_execution` → `timesheet_approved` → `qa_passed` → `customer_accepted` → `consumed` → `reconciled` (plus `revision_requested`, `cancelled`, `disputed`, `additional_credit_pending`). Overrun above reserved credits blocks consumption until additional credit approval is recorded.
5. **Customer Portal (`/api/portal/flexguard`, `/portal/flexguard`):** Strictly scoped to `(customer_id, legal_entity_id)` via `rticore_flexguard_portal_grants`. Recursive sanitizer strips all internal cost rates, employee compensation, and internal margins.
6. **SLA & Renewal Signals:** Separate tracking for acknowledgment, scope assessment, mobilization, delivery, and emergency response; read-only renewal/top-up advisories never auto-purchase.
