# 3. End-to-End Business Process Design

RTICore enforces six transactional end-to-end ERP workflows with status guards, maker-checker SoD, atomic database transactions, idempotency keys, and immutable audit trails:

1. **Lead-to-Cash:** `Lead (0005)` → `Opportunity (0003)` → `Service Estimation & 12-Phase Mandays (0053)` → `Enterprise Pricing & Snapshot Freeze (0053)` → `BoQ & Quotation DOCX` → `Submission & Negotiation` → `Finance + Legal Dual PO Verification (0012)` → `Closed Won & Auto-Handover Project (0027)` → `Delivery & BAST Acceptance` → `Billing & Collection` → `PSAK 115 Revenue Recognition & GL (0052)`.
2. **FlexGuard-to-Cash:** `Package Config (0054)` → `Contract & Versioned Conversion Matrix (0054)` → `Prepaid Invoice (Dr AR / Cr Contract Liability)` → `Credit Allocation & Lot Creation` → `14-Step Service Request Workflow` → `Credit Reservation (Available = Posted - Reserved)` → `Execution & Timesheet Approval` → `QA & Customer Acceptance (Portal/Console)` → `Credit Consumption & Release` → `Outbox Docking Event to RTI Finance (Dr Contract Liability / Cr Service Revenue)` → `Renewal Advisory`.
3. **Project-to-Profit:** `Approved Feasibility Plan (0025)` → `Project Baseline & Budget (0027)` → `Resource Assignment` → `Daily Capacity Timesheet (0033)` → `Actual Labor Cost Snapshot` → `Milestone Acceptance` → `Project Profitability & Executive BI (M01)`.
4. **Procure-to-Pay:** `Purchase Requisition & Budget Check (0042)` → `Vendor Selection` → `PO Approval (SoD)` → `Goods/Service Receipt` → `3-Way AP Invoice Match (0036)` → `Dual-Controlled Treasury Payment (0041/0052)` → `GL Posting`.
5. **Record-to-Report:** `Operational Transaction` → `Docking Outbox Event (rticore_finance_integration_events)` → `Posting Rule Resolution` → `Balanced Double-Entry Journal` → `Maker-Checker Review & Posting` → `17-Point Close Checklist (F20)` → `Statutory & Consolidated Statements (F21/F22)`.
6. **Resource-to-Revenue:** `Employee/Consultant Master (0043)` → `19-Role Approved Rate Card (0053)` → `Feasibility & Capacity Plan` → `Assignment` → `Approved Timesheet` → `Project Cost / FlexGuard Credit Consumption` → `Margin & Utilization Analytics`.
